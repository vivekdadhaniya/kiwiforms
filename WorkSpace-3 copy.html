Kiwizard AI

Create a clean, conversational lead generation form that captures high-quality prospects without overwhelming them. Start with a friendly welcome screen that clearly communicates the value (e.g., “Tell us a bit about your needs and we’ll get back with the best solution for you”). Include essential fields only: Name, Email, Company (optional), and one qualifying question (e.g., “What are you looking for?” with multiple choice or short answer). Use conditional logic to personalize follow-up questions based on responses. Keep the tone human, simple, and non-salesy. Add a short reassurance note about privacy (“No spam. Just helpful responses.”). End with a strong, clear CTA like “Get Started” or “Request Details,” and a thank-you screen that sets expectations for next steps (e.g., response time).
