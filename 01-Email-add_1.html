Accessibility Overview

Kiwiform helps you build accessible forms with an Accessibility Checker that flags issues like low contrast, missing alt text, and inaccessible embeds. Fix issues directly as you edit to meet accessibility best practices.

High contrast between the text, buttons, and background is essential for readability. Aim for a contrast ratio of 3:1 for graphics and large text, and 4.5:1 for standard-sized text.

  • Form Fields have good color contrast with the background.

  • Answers have good color contrast with the background.

  • Buttons have good color contrast with the background.

  • Button text have good color contrast with the background.

Alt text explains images and other non-text content for users with visual impairments.

  • Form Fields 5:Picture Selection images include alt text

  • Ending: Image include alt text.

Make your content accessible and understandable to all users.

Helpful Tips

Before you begin:

  • To capture new leads, include an email form field in your form and mark it as required.
  • To update existing contacts or personalize forms using HubSpot data, add an @email URL Parameter in the Logic & Flow.
  • Make sure your HubSpot account is connected and ready to receive submission data.
  • Map form fields to HubSpot Company and Deal properties. You can also assign deals to a specific pipeline and deal stage.
  • Optionally, send existing form submissions to HubSpot to syncpreviously collected data.
